About Me & Global Dev Blog

Welcome to Global Dev Blog, an independent engineering lab notebook and technical publication authored by Jihwan Park (jbjihwan).

This publication documents hands-on engineering research, real-world deployment challenges, architecture trade-offs, and empirical benchmarks in Artificial Intelligence Systems, Robotics, Deep Computer Vision, Autonomous Auditing, and Cloud Security.

👨‍💻 Who I Am​

I am an AI and Robotics Software Engineer focused on transforming theoretical algorithms into robust, verifiable edge and cloud systems. My core engineering domains include:

  • Autonomous Systems & Reinforcement Learning: Markov Decision Processes (MDP), continuous-to-discrete state-action mappings, dense velocity/distance reward shaping, and Sim-to-Real hardware transfer from Webots simulation to Arduino microcontrollers.
  • Deep Computer Vision & Real-time ITS: 30 FPS multi-stage inference pipelines, object detection (YOLOv3/v5/v8), optical tracking (CSRT, DeepSORT), deep super-resolution (EDSR), and Korean license plate OCR with temporal majority voting.
  • High-Risk NLP & Safety AI: Ordinal regression for extreme class imbalance (524:1 prior skew), weak-label calibration, monotonic probability guarantees, and deterministic sub-millisecond safety guardrails.
  • Autonomous Systems & Financial Auditing: Text-to-SQL financial reasoning, 3D sector partitioning, GraphRAG corporate cartel discovery, and automated discrepancy dossiers.
  • Full-Stack ML Engineering & Security: High-throughput FastAPI microservices, Docusaurus multi-lingual knowledge architectures, and package supply chain hardening (npm overrides).

🛡️ Editorial Policy & Verification Standards​

In an era of generic AI-generated aggregators, Global Dev Blog enforces strict engineering publication standards:

  1. 100% Empirically Tested & Reproducible:
    Every architectural pattern, SQL query, machine learning pipeline, and troubleshooting fix published on this blog is executed and verified on actual hardware rigs (NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4060 GPU, Ubuntu Linux / WSL2, Python 3.12, TypeScript, Webots) before publication.
  2. Zero Hallucination Guarantee:
    We do not publish untested theoretical code or generic AI summaries. All failure modes, benchmark figures, and exception traces are captured directly from live test harnesses and production CI/CD runs.
  3. Transparent Trade-offs & Post-mortems:
    We believe documenting failure modes—such as buffer deadlocks, cyclic dependency crashes, and Bayes prior collapses—delivers far greater value to engineering peers than idealized success stories.
  4. Accountability & Open Source:
    Articles are tied directly to verifiable GitHub repositories (github.com/jbjihwan). Readers and peer reviewers are encouraged to inspect, clone, and validate our findings.
